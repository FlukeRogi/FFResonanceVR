Welcome to the Final Fantasy Resonance VR Mod by FlukeRogi.

This mod lets you play FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE in VR. The game appears on a large virtual 3D screen in front of
you, while you are surrounded by locations from the game, such as the castle courtyard or the airship deck.

The game is shown in real 3D, and the screen behaves like a window into the game world: move your head and you
can look around and past things, just like looking through a real window.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------

- FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE, installed on Steam
- A PC VR headset, connected with Virtual Desktop or SteamVR
- A controller is recommended
- An internet connection the first time you open the mod (see below)


There is nothing else to install.


PLAYING
-------

1. Connect your headset with Virtual Desktop or SteamVR.
2. Open "FFResonanceVR.exe" in the mod folder.
3. Choose Virtual Desktop or SteamVR (SteamVR is started for you if it isn't running).
4. Press Launch.

The first time you open it, the launcher gets two things ready. This only happens once:
- It downloads UEVR, the Unreal Engine VR mod by praydog that this mod builds on, from its official GitHub page
  (about 39 MB).
- It reads the game's own fonts and artwork for the VR menu from your installed game.

The game starts through Steam as usual. VR appears about 10 seconds after the game window opens.
Your saves and Steam achievements work as normal.

Already started the game from Steam? Press "Add VR to running game" instead.


CONTROLS
--------

- VR menu: press L3 + R3 (both sticks in), or F10 on the keyboard
- In the menu: D-pad or left stick to move and change settings, A to confirm, B to close
- Re-centre the view: "Recentre" in the VR menu, or Ctrl+F12

Everything else is played exactly as normal.


THE VR MENU
-----------

- Recentre: puts the screen straight in front of you again
- Screen Distance, Size and Height: where the screen is and how big it is
- Surroundings: the location around you (Castle Courtyard, Airship Deck, or none)
- Surroundings Floor: "Seated" feels right when sitting down; "Real floor" puts the ground at your real floor
- 3D Strength: how strong the 3D effect is
- Head Parallax: how much the view changes when you move your head
- Resolution Scale: picture sharpness (higher is sharper but slower; applies the next time you launch)
- Depth of Field: the game's background blur, from off to full strength
- Battle Intro Lift: lifts the camera at the start of battles for a better view
- Battle Zoom: follows the game's camera zooms during battles
- 3D Battle Markers: shows health bars and target markers at the enemies' depth
- Reset to Defaults: puts everything back to how it started

Your settings are saved automatically.


TIPS
----

- If the game doesn't feel smooth, lower Resolution Scale or turn Depth of Field off.
- If the screen is off to one side or at the wrong height, choose Recentre in the VR menu.
- With SteamVR, Steam may show a few pop-up windows while the game starts. Just close them.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

- The launcher tells you if something is missing, for example if the game or SteamVR isn't installed.
- If VR doesn't appear, close the game and launch it again from FFResonanceVR.exe.
- To remove the mod, delete the mod folder. The game itself is never changed.
